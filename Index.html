Craft Sync Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

Craft Sync is a mobile app for handmade bath and body makers. It helps users manage inventory, recipes, costs, pricing, batch history, and production notes.

Data We Collect

Craft Sync does not collect personal data from users.

The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not send your inventory, recipes, notes, or batch history to any server.

Data Stored on Your Device

Craft Sync stores app information locally on your device, including:

  • Inventory items
  • Recipe details
  • Batch history
  • Cost and pricing information
  • Recipe notes and batch notes
  • App settings

This information stays on your device and is used only to provide the app's features.

Data Sharing

Craft Sync does not sell, share, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

Internet Access

Craft Sync does not require an account or cloud service to use its core features. The app does not upload your data to a remote server.

Backups

On Android, Craft Sync is configured to prevent app data from being included in Android cloud backup and device transfer backups.

Users may still choose to export, copy, screenshot, or manually back up information from the app outside of Craft Sync.

Children's Privacy

Craft Sync is not directed toward children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Because Craft Sync stores data locally on your device, you can delete your app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

On Android, this can usually be done from: Settings > Apps > Craft Sync > Storage > Clear storage.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Craft Sync adds new features, such as cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other services. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.
